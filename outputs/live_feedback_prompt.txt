Report ONLY a genuine learner language error: verb tense or form, subject-verb agreement, articles, prepositions, plurals, word order, missing core sentence elements (subject/verb), or a wrong word choice. Report the single most important error. Do NOT report naturalness, informal spoken register, or possible transcription problems. Do NOT guess the student's intended meaning if it is completely unclear.

Reply with json only, in exactly this shape:
