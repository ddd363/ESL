Hi, I'm Jonathan.

I'm an English teacher with over six years of experience. I work mainly with adult professionals and IELTS candidates.

Before becoming an English teacher, I worked as an electronics engineer. That experience helps me understand the communication challenges professionals face, especially when discussing technical subjects or working with international colleagues.

My teaching approach

I don't believe every student needs the same lesson. We start with your goals, identify what will make the biggest difference, and build practice around that.

You'll spend the lesson using English rather than talking about it. We'll identify what will help you most and decide what to focus on next.

You don't need to improve everything at once. The goal is steady progress in the areas that matter most.
